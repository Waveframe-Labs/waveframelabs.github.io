Independent research organization operating under the Aurora Research Initiative, developing reproducible and falsifiable scientific workflows.

Explore Projects

Waveframe Labs is the operational hub for ARI. We publish the Aurora Workflow Orchestration (AWO) method, ship the CRI-CORE tooling that ensures deterministic execution and verifiable provenance for each workflow run, and apply the stack to frontier research—e.g., Waveframe v4.0 (cosmology) and the Societal Health Simulator.

Method-first: AWO defines roles, artifacts, and falsifiability.
Deterministic tooling: CRI-CORE enforces run-level provenance.
