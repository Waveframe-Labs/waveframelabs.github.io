The research question

Capability is moving faster than accountability.

01

How was this result produced?

AI-assisted work needs a visible method, stable inputs, attributable decisions, and evidence sufficient for independent inspection.

02

Who had authority to create the consequence?

Reasoning capability does not establish permission. Authority must be explicit, current, scoped, and evaluated at the point of action.

03

What happens when certainty runs out?

Ambiguity should become a visible human decision—not an invisible machine assumption carried into production.

Research architecture

From principles to enforceable systems.

Waveframe Labs treats trustworthy AI-assisted work as a connected institutional problem—not a model feature.

01Institutional governance

Defines authority, roles, review independence, and the conditions under which work becomes admissible.

02Reproducible method

Structures human and AI contributions as inspectable artifacts with explicit gates and falsifiable claims.

03Epistemic disclosure

Makes provenance, uncertainty, transformations, and responsibility visible to downstream reviewers.

04Deterministic enforcement

Converts approved authority into machine-checkable constraints at the boundary where actions gain consequence.
