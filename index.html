Institution Overview

What is Waveframe Labs?

Waveframe Labs is an independent research institute building falsifiable, cryptographically attested AI–human research pipelines. Instead of treating AI as a black-box assistant, Waveframe treats AI activity as a first-class object of governance: logged, attributed, and audited.

The institute is organized under the Aurora Hierarchy — a six-layer architecture that runs from epistemic first principles down to concrete case studies and tools. At the core of this stack is the Neurotransparency Doctrine: the requirement that cognition, whether human or machine, must be traceable, reconstructible, and accountable.

Why Waveframe Labs Exists

Modern research increasingly relies on opaque AI systems and complex automation. At the same time, reproducibility crises and institutional bottlenecks make it hard for independent researchers to produce work that is both innovative and trustworthy.

Waveframe Labs addresses this gap by treating governance, metadata, and attestation as first-class research objects. The goal is not only to publish ideas, but to publish the process that produced them — in a way that can be audited, replayed, and challenged.

Current Focus

  • Finalizing the Neurotransparency Doctrine & Specification as a stable epistemic standard.
  • Bringing AWO into full alignment with ARI and the doctrine (v1.3.x).
  • Building the first CRI-CORE enforcement engine for AI–human workflows.
  • Demonstrating the stack through Waveframe v4.0 and the Societal Health Simulator.
