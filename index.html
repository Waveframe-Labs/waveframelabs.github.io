AI systems can now take real-world actions — moving money, deploying code, approving decisions.

Most systems detect problems or audit them after the fact.

But they don’t control the moment that actually matters: execution.

Waveframe builds systems that sit at that boundary and decide:

Should this action be allowed to execute?

Every action is evaluated and either:
✓ allowed to execute
✗ blocked before it happens

How It Works

A simple pipeline sits between a proposed action and execution:

1
Define rules — roles, approvals, and constraints
2
Normalize action — convert input into a structured proposal
3
Evaluate — CRI-CORE checks if requirements are satisfied
4
Decide — allow execution or block it
