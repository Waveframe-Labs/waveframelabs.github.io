Layer 3 — Enforcement

What is CRI-CORE?

CRI-CORE (Cognitive Research Infrastructure — CORE) is the enforcement engine for Aurora Workflow Orchestration. Its role is to make compliance with AWO and the Neurotransparency Specification mechanically checkable.

Planned Capabilities

1. Schema & Metadata Validation

Validate manifests, metadata headers, and directory structure against AWO and ARI rules.

2. Attestation & Audit Trails

Generate cryptographically verifiable logs that show which workflows ran, which models were used, and which approvals were granted.

3. Role-Aware Enforcement

Verify that builder and reviewer roles are kept separate, and that override events are explicitly recorded.

4. Compliance Badges & Reports

Emit machine-readable badges and human-readable reports summarizing whether a repository or release meets governance requirements.

Status

CRI-CORE is currently in the architecture and prototype design phase. Early functionality is being explored through standalone validators and metadata tools that will later be folded into the runtime.

Roadmap

  • Define technical architecture and component boundaries.
  • Release an MVP validator focused on manifests and metadata.
  • Integrate with the metadata pipeline and PDF Forge.
  • Expand to full role-aware enforcement and provenance snapshots.
  • Publish a public whitepaper and reference implementation.
