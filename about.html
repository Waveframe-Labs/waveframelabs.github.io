About Waveframe Labs

Independent research with a public record.

Waveframe Labs develops governance, methodology, and infrastructure for AI-assisted work that must remain reproducible, attributable, and safe to act upon.

The organization

Research that can survive inspection.

Waveframe Labs is an independent research organization operating under the Aurora Research Initiative. Its work examines how AI systems can contribute to scientific and operational processes without erasing human authority, provenance, or accountability.

The organization publishes its methods, specifications, software, case studies, and release history so claims about the work can be evaluated against durable artifacts.

Founder

Shawn C. Wright is the founder and lead researcher of Waveframe Labs. He directs the research program, develops the institutional frameworks, and coordinates the open technical ecosystem.
