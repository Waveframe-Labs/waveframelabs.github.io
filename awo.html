Layer 2 — Method

Purpose

Aurora Workflow Orchestration (AWO) is the method specification that turns the Neurotransparency Doctrine into a concrete, repeatable workflow for AI–human research.

AWO defines how projects are declared, how prompts and models are orchestrated, what logs must be kept, and how approvals and overrides are recorded.

Key Concepts

1. Project Manifest

A structured document describing what is being attempted, which models and tools are in scope, and which governance rules apply.

2. Role Separation

AWO enforces a conceptual distinction between:

  • Builder — runs workflows, prompts models, edits artefacts.
  • Reviewer — audits runs, verifies claims, approves releases.

3. Attestation & Logs

Every significant step in a workflow has an associated log entry and, where appropriate, a human or automated attestation that the step conforms to AWO and ARI rules.

4. Override & Governance Logs

When governance rules are intentionally relaxed or bypassed, AWO requires those decisions to be logged explicitly with rationale.

Alignment with Neurotransparency & ARI

AWO is not value-neutral. It is explicitly aligned with the Neurotransparency Doctrine and governed by ARI:

  • Workflows must satisfy neurotransparency requirements.
  • Metadata and logging rules are inherited from ARI policy.
  • CRI-CORE will eventually use AWO as its ground truth.

Repository & Specification

The full method specification, including sectioned requirements for manifests, logs, and validation, is maintained in the Waveframe-Labs/Aurora-Workflow-Orchestration repository.
