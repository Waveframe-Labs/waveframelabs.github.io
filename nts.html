Neurotransparency Specification (NTS)

Formal normative compliance standard for cognitive traceability in AI–human scientific workflows.

The Neurotransparency Specification (NTS) defines the mandatory disclosure, attribution, provenance, and reconstruction requirements that determine whether AI-influenced scientific artifacts meet minimum standards of epistemic legitimacy.

Where the Neurotransparency Doctrine establishes why cognitive traceability is required, NTS defines what must be disclosed—without prescribing workflows, tooling, or enforcement mechanisms.

Position in the Aurora Stack

  • Neurotransparency Specification (NTS) — normative disclosure standard (what)
