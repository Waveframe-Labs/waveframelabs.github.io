Layer 5 — Infrastructure

Tools & Infrastructure

Deterministic tooling that supports, validates, and operationalizes Aurora-aligned workflows.

Purpose

Tools enable reproducibility and auditability within the ecosystem. They do not define epistemic authority — they execute, validate, and enforce it.

Infrastructure is subordinate to governance and methodology.

Core Tooling

Glyphtrace

Symbolic epistemic compression format for representing research as compact, machine-expandable traces. Enables reconstruction, reference, and round-trip validation using minimal tokens.
