Layer 5 — Publication & Distribution

Tools

Deterministic tooling that prepares, validates, and publishes governed research artifacts. Tools do not define epistemic authority or research method; they execute and reflect decisions made upstream.

Purpose

Tools exist to make governed research reproducible, auditable, and mechanically checkable. They are subordinate to governance (L0) and method (L1) and may not redefine either.

Core Tooling

Stamp

Deterministic metadata diagnostics and remediation tooling. Stamp extracts metadata, validates it against formal schemas, normalizes errors into structured diagnostics, and separates machine-safe fixes from human judgment.

Stamp explains what is wrong and what action is required; it does not enforce, approve, or judge content.

Waveframe PDF Forge

Deterministic Markdown → PDF publication pipeline with metadata injection and provenance preservation. Forge freezes validated artifacts for citation and dissemination.

Metadata Pipeline

Conceptual pipeline spanning extraction, validation, normalization, and publication of artifact metadata. Implemented through tools such as Stamp and Forge.

Related Upstream Components

Glyphtrace (Layer 2 — Language & Ontology)

Symbolic semantic compression format used to represent claims and reasoning compactly. Glyphtrace is not a tool; it is an upstream meaning layer consumed by tooling and enforcement.
