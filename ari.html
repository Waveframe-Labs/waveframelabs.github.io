Layer 1 — Governance

Mission

The Aurora Research Initiative (ARI) is the institutional skin of Waveframe Labs. Its mission is to ensure that AI–human research conducted under the Waveframe umbrella is:

  • Epistemically grounded in the Neurotransparency Doctrine.
  • Procedurally aligned with the Aurora Workflow Orchestration (AWO) standard.
  • Enforceable through CRI-CORE and related tooling.

What ARI Owns

1. Governance Documents

ARI maintains the governance charter, role definitions, metadata policy, and invariants. These documents define the boundaries of any project claiming to run “under Aurora governance”.

2. Role Separation

ARI codifies the separation between builder and reviewer roles, and between workflow orchestration and governance approval. This separation is later enforced in AWO and CRI-CORE.

3. Approval & Override Rules

ARI defines how work is approved, when governance can override automation, and how such overrides are logged as first-class events.

Interface to AWO and CRI-CORE

ARI does not describe the step-by-step workflow of a research project. Instead, it sets the constraints under which workflows are considered legitimate. AWO then implements those constraints as a method, and CRI-CORE enforces them mechanically.

Repository & Artefacts

ARI is maintained as its own public repository within the Waveframe Labs organization. It includes:

  • Governance model and charter.
  • Neurotransparency alignment statements.
  • Metadata and logging policy.
  • Invariants and compliance expectations for tools and workflows.
