Layer 4 — Case Studies

Purpose of case studies

Case studies are not side projects; they are proofs that the Aurora stack can support serious research. Each project is expected to conform to ARI, implement AWO, pass CRI-CORE validation, and document neurotransparency in practice.

Current Flagship Projects

Waveframe v4.0 (Cosmology)

Entropy–action, observer-aware cosmology built under the AWO + CRI-CORE stack. Demonstrates how speculative theoretical work can still be reproducible and audit-ready.

  • Repository: Waveframe-v4.0

Societal Health Simulator

Streamlit-based simulator for sociotechnical dynamics and policy interventions. Serves as a sociological case study for method-driven research outside traditional institutions.

  • Repo: Societal-Health-Simulator
  • Live demo: wrightsocietalmodel.com
