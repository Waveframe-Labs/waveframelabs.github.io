Purpose

Case studies show that the Aurora Hierarchy is not just theory. Each project is an end-to-end application of the doctrine, governance, and workflow stack to a concrete scientific or sociotechnical question.

Waveframe v4.0 — Cosmology

A cosmological model exploring spacetime and expansion through an information-first lens. Waveframe v4.0 acts as the flagship case study for applying AWO to theoretical physics and simulation-based research.

Repository: Waveframe-Labs/Waveframe-v4.0
Status: active development under Aurora governance.

Societal Health Simulator (SHS)

A sociotechnical modelling project exploring how institutional, cultural, and informational variables affect societal health over time. SHS demonstrates AWO and governance in a domain closer to policy and social science.

Repository: Waveframe-Labs/Societal-Health-Simulator

Tauwarp & Waveframe Lineage (Archived)

Earlier explorations of time reparameterization, emergent cosmology, and Waveframe v3.x are preserved as research artefacts. They provide historical context for how the current governance and workflow stack emerged.

Repositories: Wright-Shawn/Tauwarp, Wright-Shawn/waveframe-v3, Wright-Shawn/waveframe-v3.1, and related archives.

New case studies will be added as additional projects are brought under the Aurora stack.
