Glyphtrace v0.0.1 Released

Date: 2025-12-26 · Scope: Tokenization & Reasoning Compression Layer

What changed

  • Introduced Glyphtrace as a universal research-compression format.
  • Standardized token representation for conceptual lineage.
  • Added Concept DOI and first version DOI.

Why it matters

Glyphtrace establishes the compression primitive for AWO, CRI-CORE, and research reproducibility. It enables claim lineage, semantic traceability, and compact publication encoding.
