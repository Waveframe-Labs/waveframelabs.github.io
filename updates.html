Neurotransparency Specification (NTS) v2.0.0 Released

Date: 2025-12-18 · Scope: Layer 0 — Normative Specification (Neurotransparency)

What changed

  • Formalized the Neurotransparency Specification as a standalone normative standard.
  • Clarified separation between doctrine (epistemic rationale) and specification (disclosure requirements).
  • Defined explicit compliance targets for downstream workflow and enforcement systems.

Why it matters

This release establishes a machine-verifiable standard for cognitive traceability in AI-assisted scientific work. Where the Neurotransparency Doctrine defines why transparency is required, the Specification defines what must be disclosed for work to be considered epistemically legitimate under Aurora governance.

The separation prevents category errors between philosophy, governance, methodology, and enforcement, enabling auditability without exposing internal cognition or delegating epistemic authority to machines.

Neurotransparency Doctrine (NTD) Released

Date: 2025-12-17 · Scope: Layer 0 — Epistemic Doctrine (Neurotransparency)

What changed

  • Formalized the Neurotransparency Doctrine as the epistemic foundation for AI–human scientific work.
  • Defined cognitive traceability as a requirement for legitimacy, not correctness or outcome quality.
  • Established separation between epistemic rationale (doctrine) and normative disclosure (specification).

Why it matters

The Doctrine establishes why AI-assisted research requires traceability, attribution, and bounded authority. It frames transparency as a prerequisite for accountability rather than a proxy for truth.

By anchoring transparency at the epistemic layer, the Doctrine prevents enforcement, tooling, or automation from acquiring implicit scientific authority.

Aurora Research Initiative (ARI) Formalized

Date: 2025-12-16 · Scope: Layer 1 — Institutional Governance

What changed

  • Established ARI as the institutional authority governing the Aurora ecosystem.
  • Defined binding governance over standards, methodologies, enforcement systems, and case studies.
  • Clarified authority boundaries between doctrine, specification, workflows, and enforcement.

Why it matters

ARI defines who decides within the ecosystem. It prevents individual tools, workflows, or automation from implicitly acquiring epistemic power.

By separating governance from implementation, ARI enables reproducibility without centralization or delegated authority.
